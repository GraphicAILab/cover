## AI-Powered Automation

Our AI solutions are driving efficiency across various automatable fields, particularly in text management, by streamlining document classification and process workflows. We have already delivered practical applications in government RFP generation, automated quotation systems, and end-to-end order processing. Concurrently, we are expanding our real-world footprint through integration with physical equipment; this includes automated harvesting in agriculture—where robotic arms and vision models work in seamless synergy—and high-speed logistics parcel sorting.

### RFP generation

Bidding documents are often lengthy, complex, and filled with highly repetitive materials. Historically, managing them was a major headache—a highly time-consuming task with very low returns. Today, we can leverage AI tools to extract key insights from cluttered materials while automatically generating highly repetitive content. This provides massive support in streamlining the bidding process and accelerating document preparation.

### automated quotation systems

"In quotation management, clients often lack deep industry knowledge. This forces companies—especially in pharma and manufacturing—to spend significant effort processing quotes due to highly complex formulas and manufacturing processes. Our solution integrates databases with AI tools to instantly generate quotation reports, offering clients clear price ranges and solid pricing rationale. This fast-turnaround capability provides critical scalability for managing high-volume client requests

### end-to-end order processing

Logistics hubs like ports, terminals, and transfer stations handle overwhelming cargo volumes that require initial staging. Packages must first undergo primary sorting based on regional destinations before moving to fine-grained sorting. This initial stage is heavily labor-intensive due to the massive throughput. Our solution automates parcel data logging and leverages AI to manage and execute this preliminary sorting, delivering a massive boost to overall operational efficiency.

We are also accelerating real-world deployment by integrating our AI models with physical equipment, specifically targeting New Zealand’s agricultural sector with solutions like automated kiwifruit and avocado picking machines. Given the extreme seasonality of farming, labor shortages during peak harvest windows are a critical pain point.
