### Game & VR & AR

Game: As a specialized computer graphics company, we streamline production for the gaming industry by offering an end-to-end asset pipeline driven by our generative 3D AI. Our product enables studios to instantly generate high-quality 3D meshes and textures from text prompts or images. Crucially, our real-time rendering tech and custom cross-platform shaders allow games to toggle seamlessly between absolute photorealism and highly stylized animation aesthetics—such as anime or cell shading—ensuring flawless performance and high frame rates regardless of the artistic direction.

VR & AR : For the rapidly evolving VR and AR sectors, we focus on developing highly customized products tailored to specific hardware and spatial requirements. Rather than offering generic software, we build bespoke interactive applications and custom rendering pipelines that address the unique constraints of spatial computing. Whether creating tailored enterprise VR training simulations or branded consumer AR experiences, our solutions are engineered from the ground up to integrate unique client features, maximize specific hardware capabilities, and deliver exact business outcomes.

Driving these custom VR and AR products is our specialized graphics expertise in runtime optimization and environmental blending. To eliminate motion sickness in our custom VR systems, we optimize rendering pipelines to guarantee ultra-low latency and flawless 90Hz to 120Hz refresh rates. For custom AR integration, we develop tailored real-time lighting estimation and shadow-mapping algorithms that analyze live camera feeds on the fly, locking virtual objects into physical spaces with perfectly matched ambient reflections and zero drift.

### Film and Video

Film & Video : In the film and television sector, we provide bespoke visual effects and graphics development tailored to the unique creative needs of each production. Leveraging our deep computer graphics expertise, we build custom rendering pipelines, advanced shaders, and tailored VFX solutions that allow directors to achieve precise artistic visions. Whether it is generating cinematic-grade environments or crafting specialized digital assets, our tailored approach ensures seamless integration into high-end film production workflows.

Short Video : For the fast-paced short video market, we deliver automated, customized video generation products designed for rapid content creation. By integrating cutting-edge generative models, our platform allows brands and creators to instantly produce high-quality short-form videos with customized styles, templates, and visual layouts. This enables scalable video marketing and rapid content iteration while maintaining a highly professional aesthetic.

AI-Driven Pipelines : To provide a truly comprehensive media solution, our products incorporate advanced AI capabilities that automate the most labor-intensive parts of production. Our tools feature AI-driven music generation and automated voiceovers (dubbing) that sync perfectly with the visual pacing. Furthermore, we utilize intelligent AI rigging to automatically handle skeletal animation and motion binding for 3D characters, dramatically reducing the time and technical barrier required to bring digital figures to life.

### Simulation Environment Generation

Simulation Environment Generation: For the fields of embodied AI and autonomous driving, we provide comprehensive synthetic data generation services within highly realistic simulation environments. Training these cutting-edge models requires massive volumes of diverse data, which is often difficult, dangerous, or costly to collect in the real world. Our platform solves this by automatically generating paired datasets—such as perfectly matched 3D sensor logs, camera feeds, and ground-truth annotations—allowing developers to efficiently train, test, and validate their AI models across millions of simulated edge-case scenarios.
