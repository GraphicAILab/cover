## 3D Model Generation Tool

We present a generative 3D model capable of generating corresponding 3D models and textures from input images, offering flexible asset generation via text, single images, or multi-view captures. Operating locally, the model enables rapid, high-volume generation of diverse 3D assets within a tight timeframe, significantly simplifying the conventional 3D pipeline. For 3D printing, the toy industry, and static-environment simulation tasks—such as environment training for autonomous driving and embodied AI—this solution already delivers production-ready results.
